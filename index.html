VAYYU — Indian Hip-Hop Artist from Lucknow, India.

Rooted in the sound and culture of Lucknow, VAYYU brings together melodic rap, modern hip-hop and contemporary trap influences.

Inspired by the city's cypher culture and its distinctive Zubaan-e-Lucknow, his music reflects ambition, relationships, personal journeys and the world around him.

Building a sound of his own. Representing where he comes from.
