VAYYU — INDIAN HIP-HOP ARTIST FROM LUCKNOW, INDIA.

VAYYU is the artist identity of Rudra Yadav, a rapper and songwriter from Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India. Building his sound from the city, VAYYU brings together modern hip-hop, melodic rap, trap and drill.

Beginning his music journey in 2025, he continues to shape a sound rooted in honest expression, ambition and the energy of his surroundings. Every release adds another chapter to the VAYYU story.
