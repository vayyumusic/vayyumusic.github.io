03 / THE ARTIST
WHO IS
VAYYU?

VAYYU (born Rudra Yadav on 26 August 2007) is an Indian hip-hop artist, rapper and songwriter from Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India. Known for his blend of melodic rap, modern trap and drill, VAYYU brings together contemporary hip-hop sounds with personal storytelling, ambition and self-expression. Rooted in the musical identity of Lucknow, VAYYU represents a new generation of artists emerging from the city's evolving hip-hop scene. His artistic journey is connected to the spirit of local cyphers and grassroots hip-hop culture, including the creative movement surrounding Zubaan-e-Lucknow. Drawing inspiration from the streets, culture and voice of his hometown, he aims to contribute to the growing recognition of Lucknow and Uttar Pradesh within Indian hip-hop. Through his music, VAYYU explores themes of relationships, personal experiences, ambition and individuality, developing a distinctive sound shaped by regional roots and contemporary global hip-hop influences. Under the identity of VAYYU WORLD, he continues to build his musical catalogue, collaborate with emerging artists and establish his presence in India's independent hip-hop landscape.
