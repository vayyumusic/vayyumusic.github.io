THE MUSIC.

VAYYU, born Rudra Yadav, is an Indian hip-hop artist, rapper and songwriter from Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India.

Rooted in the city's evolving hip-hop culture, his artistic journey reflects a connection to the creative energy of local cyphers and communities, including the culture surrounding Zubaan-e-Lucknow.

With a blend of melodic expression, modern production and contemporary hip-hop influences, VAYYU continues to develop a sound and identity of his own.
