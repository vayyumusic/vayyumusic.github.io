VAYYU is an Indian Hip-Hop artist, rapper and songwriter from Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India.

Rooted in the identity of his city and connected to the creative energy surrounding Lucknow's hip-hop community, his musical journey reflects a blend of melodic rap, modern trap and contemporary hip-hop.

Inspired by the culture of local cyphers and movements such as Zubaan-e-Lucknow, VAYYU represents a growing generation of voices shaping the city's musical identity.

From personal stories to ambition, every release adds another chapter to the VAYYU WORLD.
