VAYYU — Indian Hip-Hop Artist from Lucknow, India.

VAYYU, born Rudra Yadav, is an Indian hip-hop artist, rapper and songwriter from Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India. He began his musical journey in 2025.

His sound blends melodic rap with modern hip-hop, trap and drill influences, creating music shaped by personal experiences, ambition, relationships and the journey of self-discovery.

Rooted in Lucknow's local hip-hop and cypher culture, his artistic identity also draws inspiration from the city's distinctive Zubaan-e-Lucknow.

Through VAYYU WORLD, he continues to build his musical identity while representing his hometown and developing a sound of his own.
